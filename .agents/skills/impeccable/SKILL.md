# Impeccable: Zero-Defect Visual Craftsmanship & Anti-AI-Slop

Impeccable guarantees that every UI element looks human-crafted by a world-class product designer. It strictly forbids lazy AI design tropes and enforces mathematical precision and emotional resonance.

## 1. Absolute Ban on AI Flops (Anti-Slop Manifest)

❌ **BANNED AI PATTERNS:**
- **Generic AI Gradients:** Random purple-to-blue or neon rainbow gradients that look like generic templates.
- **Floating Unanchored Cards:** Cards with blurry, excessive drop shadows that float aimlessly without depth hierarchy.
- **Uniform 3-Column Feature Grids:** Repetitive feature blocks with generic icon bubbles.
- **Placeholder Text & Fake Data:** "Lorem Ipsum", "Sample Song Title", or dummy values when real domain data exists.
- **Sub-44px Touch Targets:** Tiny clickable links or buttons that are impossible to tap with a thumb on stage or mobile.
- **Jarring Light Flashes on Dark Mode:** Light-colored modals or popups flashing over a pitch-black stage view.
- **Misaligned Chord Overlays:** Chords rendered in proportional fonts that drift away from their associated lyric syllables.

## 2. Craftsmanship Standards for Selah

### A. The "Sacred & Tech" Design Language
- **Background Palette:** True Stage Pitch Black (`#000000`), secondary `#0A0A0C`, elevated card `#111114`, frosted glass `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Accent Hierarchy:** Warm liturgical gold (`#D4AF37` / `#d4a574`), subtle gold wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`), active service emerald (`#10B981` / `#34D399`).
- **Borders:** Ultra-fine subtle dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`). Never thick harsh borders.

### B. Typography Discipline
- **Display / Hymnal Headings:** `font-serif` (Cormorant Garamond) for song titles, section labels, and sacred accents.
- **UI Body & Controls:** `font-sans` (Inter) with crisp letter-spacing (`tracking-tight` on large type, `tracking-wide` on micro-labels).
- **Chords & Music Notation:** `font-mono` (JetBrains Mono) ALWAYS. Chords must be monospaced and visually aligned with lyric syllable anchors.

### C. Touch Ergonomics & Stage Readiness
- All interactive controls must have `min-h-[44px]` and `min-w-[44px]` touch targets.
- Stage controls must be reachable in one-handed thumb zone.
- Contrast must meet WCAG AAA for dim stage environments (minimum 7:1 contrast for chords and lyrics).
- Optical balance: Use visual centering rather than pure geometric centering for icons with asymmetric weight.
